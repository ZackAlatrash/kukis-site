1. The service

Kukis is a Shopify app. It adds storefront widgets (Save my cart, Save this for later, Wishlist and Rejection Recovery) that let shoppers email themselves a cart, a product or a list, with an optional, unticked box for marketing consent. Kukis sends the email the shopper asked for, keeps a record of the consent they gave or refused, and shows you the results in the Shopify admin.

Kukis is not a full email-marketing platform and not a cookie banner. It works alongside the banner and the email tools you already use.

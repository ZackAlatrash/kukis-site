Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Kukis is a Shopify app. Shoppers use it to email themselves a cart, a saved product or a wishlist, and merchants use it to keep a record of the marketing consent those shoppers gave. This page explains what we do with personal data, both as a business and on behalf of the merchants who install the app.

Two different roles

  • For shopper data, we are the processor. The merchant whose store you shopped in decides what is collected and why. We act on that merchant's instructions under the data processing agreement below.
  • For merchant data, we are the controller. That covers the account details of the shop that installs Kukis, and anyone who contacts us.

If you are a shopper and want your data corrected or deleted, contact the store you bought from. They can action it in Kukis, and Shopify's own privacy requests reach us automatically.

Kukis sets no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It sets one strictly necessary cookie, cc_wishlist_id, and only when a shopper uses the wishlist, so their list can be shown back to them. It lasts one year.

The marketing checkbox is never ticked in advance. If a shopper leaves it unticked, we record that refusal too, because a merchant has to be able to show what was asked and what was answered.

What we process as a business (merchant data)

  • Shop domain, store name, the sender name and addresses configured in the app, and the app's settings.
  • The Shopify access token for the shop, held so the app can work.
  • Billing is handled by Shopify. We never see card details.
  • Messages you send us, for example a demo request or a support email.

Our lawful basis is the contract with the merchant, and our legitimate interest in running and securing the service.

The application and its database run in the EU. Email is sent through a US provider, so email content and recipient addresses are processed in the United States under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We say this plainly rather than claim that all data stays in the EU, because it does not.

How the data is protected

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest at our hosting providers.
  • Unsubscribe and wishlist links are random tokens, stored only as hashes.
  • Logs are scrubbed of email addresses, tokens and keys before they are written.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it, each with their own account and two-factor login.
  • We keep a written security incident response procedure, and tell affected merchants without undue delay.

Retention and deletion

Merchants choose their own retention windows in the app, within limits of 30 to 2,555 days. When a window elapses, cart contents, IP addresses and user agents are cleared, delivery history is deleted, and consent records are deleted at the end of their own, longer window. Uninstalling the app starts Shopify's 48-hour redaction process, after which the shop's data is erased.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and you can object to processing. Shoppers should ask the store they bought from; merchants can email us directly. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
